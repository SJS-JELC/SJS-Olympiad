# Readiness pilot and entry redesign

Status: implemented and verified locally; not deployed.

The approved design replaces permanent closure and numbered entries with reversible Pilot on/off, unnumbered browser attempts and access confirmation on school GO. Schools come from the current unfinished event's linked nonremoved teams; directory-only schools are excluded. History persists across mode and event changes.

Landing is competitor-only with a four-digit masked input and illustrated GO; only its eagle exposes teacher authentication in a password speech bubble. Pilot mode shows welcome copy and GO without a pupil PIN. School selection follows the team chooser; Funny Bones follows the standard question layout with personal best and three distinct school names ranked by each school's best score. No manual leaderboard controls or new-attempt action.

School Readiness reports School, Access Confirmation Time and High Score per attempt, allowing repeated school names. Teacher controls are revision guarded; entry/submission share the mode fence. Credentials and drawings remain private, and competition scoring and claims are independent.

Local execution, no delegates. Preserve existing work. No deployment authorised. See [redesign ticket](issues/03-pilot-redesign.md), [current guide](../../docs/readiness-pilot.md) and [verification](verification.md).
